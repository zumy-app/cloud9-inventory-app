Cloud 9 Team Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Cloud 9 Team is provided by Cloud 9 Market for authorized staff. This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it to manage inventory and print shelf labels.

Information we process

Account. Your Odoo username and password are used to sign in. They are stored in the device secure storage and sent over HTTPS to the Cloud 9 Odoo server at admin.cloud9market.net. The password is never written to logs.

Inventory. Barcodes, product names, quantities, prices, expiry dates, categories and notes that you scan or enter are sent to the Cloud 9 Odoo server so the store inventory stays up to date.

Device settings. The selected Bluetooth printer address, print darkness, language preference and recent category choices are stored locally on the device.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan product barcodes. Frames are processed on the device by the barcode scanner and are not stored or sent to us. The app does not access photos or files.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to connect to a label printer that you have already paired in Android settings. The app does not scan for nearby devices and does not use Bluetooth to determine location.

How we use information

We use the information to sign you in, load and update inventory, print labels, remember your settings, provide support, and protect the service. We do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We do not sell your information. Inventory and account data are stored in Cloud 9 Market's Odoo system and may be handled by service providers that host or support that system. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect the service. The app has no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. If you use Share TSV, the export is handed to the app you choose and that app's privacy policy applies.

Retention and deletion

Account and inventory records are kept according to Cloud 9 Market policy. Local settings and cached session data are removed when you sign out or uninstall the app. Accounts are created and managed by Cloud 9 Market. To request deletion of your account or a copy of your data, contact your store administrator or the support contact shown on the Google Play listing.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Sign-in credentials are kept in the Android secure storage. Access to the Odoo server is limited to authorized accounts.

Children

This is a workforce app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at the privacy policy link in the Google Play listing.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact your Cloud 9 Market administrator or the support contact shown on the Google Play listing.
