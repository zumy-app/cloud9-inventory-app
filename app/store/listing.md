# Cloud 9 Team — Play Store listing copy

## App name (30 max)
Cloud 9 Team Inventory

## Short description (80 max)
Scan, count, and print shelf labels for Cloud 9 Market.

## Full description
Cloud 9 Team is the inventory and shelf-label app for authorized Cloud 9 Market
staff.

- Scan barcodes to look up products, receive stock, and update counts.
- Add new products with price, cost, quantity, category, and expiry date.
- Review a batch of shelf labels before printing.
- Print 48 × 25 mm shelf labels on a paired Bluetooth thermal printer.
- Works in English and Spanish.

Sign-in is required. Accounts are created and managed by Cloud 9 Market; this
app is not for general public use. The app contains no ads and no analytics.

## App access (Play Console → App content → App access)
All functionality requires a Cloud 9 Odoo account.

1. Open the app and sign in with the provided test username and password.
2. Tap **Labels** to see the label batch and the print controls.
3. Tap the printer icon to open the Bluetooth printer sheet.
